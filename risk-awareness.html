Risk Awareness

Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile and unpredictable.

Prices can rise quickly, but they can also fall without warning. Participation in crypto carries the possibility of losing part or all of your capital.

ZERO does not promise financial return. ZERO does not provide trading signals. ZERO does not offer investment advice.

ZERO exists as a reminder that risk is always present in speculative markets.

Many participants enter crypto with optimism and confidence. Few prepare for loss.

Understanding risk is not fear. It is clarity.

ZERO encourages reflection before participation, and awareness before commitment.

Every decision in crypto should be made independently, with personal responsibility and proper research.

If ZERO serves any purpose, it is to remind participants that every cycle contains uncertainty, and every asset carries risk.

ZERO is not a guarantee. ZERO is a perspective.
