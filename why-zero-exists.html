Why ZERO Exists

Crypto is built on hope.

Hope that technology will change finance. Hope that early believers will be rewarded. Hope that this cycle will be different from the last.

Yet history repeats itself. Markets rise with excitement, and fall with silence.

ZERO exists as a reminder of that cycle.

ZERO is not designed to promise profit. ZERO does not teach trading. ZERO does not guide investment decisions.

ZERO exists to represent a psychological truth: every crypto journey carries the possibility of returning to zero.

In a market filled with noise, hype, and predictions, ZERO stands as a quiet anchor.

It invites reflection before action. It encourages awareness before commitment. It values honesty over illusion.

ZERO is not about price. ZERO is about perspective.

Because sometimes, understanding loss is the beginning of understanding value.
